The Townes at Union Village sit beside the Henderson Hospital campus. The sales office is at 1001 Gearus Place, Henderson, NV 89011. Henderson Hospital is at 1050 W. Galleria Drive, Henderson, NV 89011.

Dr. Jan Duffy represents the buyer. Century Communities is the builder. Tell the sales office you have an agent on the first visit.

Century listed three plans as of August 29, 2026. Residence 1405 starts at $335,990. Residence 1478 starts at $339,990. Residence 1479 starts at $352,990. Each plan lists 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and a 2-car garage. Prices move with the homesite, options, and incentives. Confirm the number before you write.

Henderson Medical Plaza is at 825 N. Gibson Road, on the same hospital campus. If your shift is at a different hospital, Dr. Jan will time that drive from Gearus Place. This page does not publish a minute count.

Tours are by appointment, seven days a week. Call (702) 500-1955 and name the shift you are coming off.

These townhomes are open to every qualified buyer. Occupation is not a condition of sale.
